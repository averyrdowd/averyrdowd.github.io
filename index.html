Avery R. Dowd VISUAL DESIGNER
Based in New York City, I recently graduated from FIT with a BFA in Graphic Design.

I’m interested in branding, typography, editorial design, and the spaces where print and digital design meet. My experience in hospitality and cultural institutions has shaped the way I think about design as part of a larger experience, from the menu someone holds to the identity surrounding a museum.

With a background in French and the History of Art, I’m especially interested in the cultural and historical context behind visual communication.

Outside of design, I spend a lot of time traveling, taking photographs, listening to music, and reading.

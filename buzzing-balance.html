Buzzing Balance MOTION GRAPHICS
DESCRIPTION
Bees are essential to human life and survival. They are a dying species due to parasites and diseases, pesticide exposure, habitat loss and poor nutrition, and climate change. Bees are team players; if just a few colony members are impacted, the whole hive may fail.

In an attempt to bring awareness to their importance and role. Buzzing Balance was animated to educate and entertain. Each character was drawn and animated to bring viewers through an informative story.

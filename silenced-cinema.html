Silenced Cinema BRAND IDENTITY
DESCRIPTION
A series consisting of three triptychs and a short animation advertising a film festival only showing films that were banned around the world. Each poster represents a day of the festival and shares what will happen. The bold, hidden terms noted focus on the overall themes covered.

The campagin focuses each series how communities that are impacted by censorship can't see, what they can't hear, and what they can't share respectively. These series' are acompagnied by a video advertisement continuing the motif of the moving eye revealing and hiding information.

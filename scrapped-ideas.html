Scrapped Ideas SOCIAL MEDIA
DESCRIPTION
As part of the Adobe Creative Apprenticeship Program, I worked with Adobe to create a social media short sharing how I use Firefly Boards in my design process.

The final design took form as an advertisement for an exhibition sharing sketches and concepts from famous artists and designers that didn't make it to the final stage. By understanding the thought process behind the greats, viewers can gain a better understanding of process, experimentation, and trial and error. In turn, the project shows how Firefly Boards can be used to organize ideas and streamline the creative process.

Museum of Arts and Design BRANDING
DESCRIPTION
During my internship with the Museum of Arts and Design, I worked with the Development team to create print and digital materials supporting membership, fundraising, events, and visitor engagement. Projects ranged from event menus and signage to sponsorship materials, rental brochures, membership forms, and other donor-facing collateral.

Working within MAD’s existing visual identity, I adapted the brand across a wide range of formats and audiences. I also developed a series of icons for each membership level, creating a visual system that could be used across membership materials and later expanded into pieces distributed directly to museum members.
